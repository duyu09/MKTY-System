## Document Overview

**Project Title:** *Minh Khoe Tue Y — Design and Implementation of a Health Management and Diagnostic Assistance System Based on LLMs and Multimodal Artificial Intelligence*
(*Abbreviation: MKTY Smart Healthcare System*)

**Chinese:** 明康慧医——基于LLM与多模态人工智能的健康管理与辅助诊疗系统的设计与实现
**Vietnamese:** *Minh Khỏe Tuệ Y – Thiết kế và triển khai hệ thống quản lý sức khỏe và hỗ trợ chẩn đoán y tế dựa trên LLM và trí tuệ nhân tạo đa mô thức (Tên viết tắt: MKTY – Hệ Thống Y Liệu Trí Tuệ)*

### Project Documentation Description

* **File `MKTY-Paper.pdf`:**
  The original undergraduate thesis submitted to the *Faculty of Computer Science and Technology, Qilu University of Technology (Shandong Academy of Sciences)*, Class of 2025.
  **Thesis Title:** *Design and Implementation of a Health Management and Diagnostic Assistance System Based on LLMs and Multimodal Artificial Intelligence.*

* **File `MKTY-PPT.pdf`:**
  The defense and outstanding-thesis presentation slides (PDF version) for the above thesis.

* **File `Outstanding_Thesis_Evaluation_Form.pdf`:**
  The official evaluation form for the “Outstanding Bachelor’s Thesis” selection at *Qilu University of Technology (Shandong Academy of Sciences)*.

* **Directory `Developer-Guide-DeepWiki-EN`:**
  Contains **23 detailed English developer guides (PDF files)** providing comprehensive technical explanations and diagrams of the project.
  These materials are hosted and versioned via the [DeepWiki](https://deepwiki.com/duyu09/MKTY-System) platform.
